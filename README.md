## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Does PM Surya Ghar create jobs beyond installation?

Yes. Beyond rooftop fitting, the scheme generates work in module manufacturing, logistics, sales, subsidy paperwork, inspection, and long-term maintenance. Many of these roles sit in smaller cities, which spreads employment well beyond metro solar hubs and increases competition for experienced supervisors and technicians.

### Q2. Do solar installers need formal certification?

Certification is strongly preferred rather than universally mandated for every role. Employers increasingly favour technicians trained through recognised programmes such as Suryamitra, because certified crews handle electrical safety, working at height and grid-connection requirements with fewer errors, fewer callbacks and better customer reviews.

A blend usually works best. Core roles such as design engineers and site supervisors suit permanent hiring, while fitters and helpers can be engaged per project. This balance protects margins during slow months and still keeps knowledge inside the company.
